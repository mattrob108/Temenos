Common questions

FAQ

What is the mandala?

A 9-fold geometry — four core petals plus five Pro petals — that grows as you weave more systems into your self-knowledge.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is stored in your own row, protected by row-level security, and never shared. You can edit or delete at any time.

What if I don’t know my birth time?

Make your best guess — we’ll flag the profile as approximate. You can update the exact time later in settings, and your systems will recompute.

Why these particular systems?

They’re complementary lenses on the self — astrological, energetic, archetypal, somatic. Together they form a richer portrait than any single system alone.
